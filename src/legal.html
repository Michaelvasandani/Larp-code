larp-code Legal and About

larp-code is an independent, popup-only accountability companion for two adults sharing a time-bounded NeetCode 150 Challenge. Its single purpose is to record cooperative, self-attested progress and show the derived shared Grovekin Pet state. The catalog is a reviewed, immutable import; future corrections create a new Problem Set Version.

Non-affiliation

larp-code is an independent product and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by NeetCode or LeetCode. NeetCode and LeetCode are referenced only to identify the third-party study list and problem destinations used by members.

Third-party catalog notice

Catalog source: neetcode-gh/leetcode, commit 5f9dbb6030c8243c933b799f7999092183d258d1, data file .problemSiteData.json.
